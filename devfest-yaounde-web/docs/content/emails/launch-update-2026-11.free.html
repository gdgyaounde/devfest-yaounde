What's changing before Saturday

Hi! You've reserved your free spot for DevFest Yaoundé 2026 — brilliant, we can't wait to see you. A few updates before the day:

1

One day. DevFest Yaoundé 2026 is on Saturday 21 November 2026. One day, the whole community.

2

Entry is free. Your free pass gets you into the talks, the workshops (within each room's capacity) and the sponsor stands — and you leave with a certificate of participation.

3

What's for paid passes. So it's clear from now: food, drinks, swag and event identifiers (badge, lanyard, bracelet or similar) are for paid passes, including if there's some left at the end of the day. It isn't about who deserves what — it's exactly what paid passes pay for. So do plan something to eat and drink.

4

Fancy the full package? You can still get a paid pass until Wednesday 18 November at 23:59 (Yaoundé time).

The speakers are announced! 24 talks and workshops on the programme — have a look at the lineup. And for every detail, our participation terms.

What to bring? Yourself, a valid ID, a laptop if you're following a workshop, and something to eat and drink.

See you Saturday!
